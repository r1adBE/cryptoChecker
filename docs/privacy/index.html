5. Sichern und Wiederherstellen

Mit «Sichern & Wiederherstellen» exportiert die App Merkliste (inkl. deiner Notizen und Gruppen), Alarme, Favoriten, Portfolio und Einstellungen als JSON-Datei an einen Ort, den du selbst wählst – unter Android über die Android-Dateiauswahl (z. B. «Downloads»), unter iOS über den Dialog der App «Dateien» (z. B. «Auf meinem iPhone» oder ein Speicheranbieter deiner Wahl wie iCloud Drive). Beim Wiederherstellen liest die App nur die Datei, die du dort auswählst. Die App sendet diese Datei nirgendwohin. Wählst du einen Cloud-Speicher, gelten dessen Datenschutzbestimmungen. Auf Wunsch schützt du die Datei mit einem Passwort: Sie wird dann auf deinem Gerät verschlüsselt (AES-256-GCM, Schlüssel per PBKDF2 aus dem Passwort); das Passwort wird weder gespeichert noch übertragen, und ohne es lässt sich die Sicherung nicht wiederherstellen. Sicherungsdateien sind zwischen Android und iOS austauschbar.

Stichtag-Export: Den Wert deines Portfolios an einem Datum speichert die App als CSV-Datei ebenfalls nur an dem Ort, den du im Dateidialog wählst. Die Datei wird nicht übertragen.

Analyses such as “⚡ Unusual Activity” or “💡 Why is this moving?” are computed on your device from this public market data. No data is sent to the developer or to any AI or analytics service for this.

For the mini chart in the watchlist, the value history of the portfolio widget (only for coins whose hourly prices are still missing, at most once an hour) and the cut-off export the app loads public daily or hourly prices from Binance (including its market data mirror, futures and Binance.US) or Coinbase Exchange, and exchange rates from Frankfurter for the conversion. Only the trading pair or currency and the date are requested – never your holdings or portfolio values. Conversion and the valuation of your portfolio are calculated exclusively on your device.

5. Backup and restore

With “Backup & restore” the app exports your watchlist (including your notes and groups), alarms, favorites, portfolio and settings as a JSON file to a location you choose yourself – on Android via the Android file picker (e.g. “Downloads”), on iOS via the Files app dialog (e.g. “On My iPhone” or a storage provider of your choice such as iCloud Drive). When restoring, the app only reads the file you pick there. The app does not send this file anywhere. If you choose a cloud storage, its privacy terms apply. You can optionally protect the file with a password: it is then encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from the password with PBKDF2); the password is neither stored nor transmitted, and without it the backup cannot be restored. Backup files can be exchanged between Android and iOS.

Cut-off export: The value of your portfolio on a date is saved as a CSV file only to the location you choose in the file dialog. The file is not transmitted.

6. Notifications, speech output and widgets

Notifications, alarms (including portfolio alarms) and widgets are generated entirely on the device; no push service is used (no Apple Push Notification service on iOS, no Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android). On iOS the app can deliver alarms as Time Sensitive notifications so that they also appear while a Focus is active; you can turn this off in iOS settings. With “Hide amounts” the portfolio, the portfolio widget and portfolio alarms show no amounts.

Custom alarm sound (Android): If you pick your own audio file as the alarm sound, the app copies it on your device into the media library (folder “Notifications/CryptoChecker”) so that Android can play it for notifications. The file stays on the device and is not transmitted; you can delete it there at any time. On iOS only the bundled sounds are available.

For speech output the app uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your Android device (e.g. “Speech Services by Google”) or the built-in iOS system voices. The text to be spoken (price, alarm) is handed to that engine; how it processes the text depends on its provider. The app itself neither stores nor transmits these texts.

Widgets read their data from the app’s local storage (on iOS from the App Group container, see section 3) and fetch prices directly from the providers listed above when needed.
