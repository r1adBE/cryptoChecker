Market data shown by the apps is fetched at runtime from third-party APIs (for

terms of those providers. It is not part of the Software and is not covered by
the MIT License.

Foreign exchange reference rates

Reference rates come from the European Central Bank (source: ECB), the National
Bank of Ukraine (source: National Bank of Ukraine) and the National Bank of
Poland (source: Narodowy Bank Polski). Rates against other currencies (cross
rates) and the change against the previous publication are calculated by the
apps from these rates; the apps mark such values as converted.

Alarm sounds

The four iOS alarm sounds (cc_chime, cc_crystal, cc_dingdong, cc_pulse) are
synthesised by ios/tools/gen_alarm_sounds.py (no third-party audio) and are
dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0).
