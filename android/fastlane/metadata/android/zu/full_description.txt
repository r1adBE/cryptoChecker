I-Crypto Checker ihlola izintengo ku-exchange yakho, ikwazise uma ukuhlola kuthola izinga lakho. Uma i-app ivaliwe, i-Android inquma isikhathi sokuhlola – kanye ku-15 min ubuningi, kaningi emva kwamahora. Ukweqa okufushane kungaphuthelwa; i-app iyasho. Intengo ye-exchange yakho, hhayi isilinganiso – mahhala, ayikho i-akhawunti.

OKUHLUKILE
• I-exchange yakho, hhayi isilinganiso: umugqa ngamunye unentengo yokugcina ku-exchange oyikhethile – Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Bybit nokunye.
• I-alamu eyazisa kanye: “Ngitshele uma i-BTC yeqa ku-100,000 USDT.” Ikhala uma ukuhlola kuthola izinga leqiwe, hhayi ekubuyekezeni ngakunye – “Kanye” iyayivala, “Njalo” iyayivula futhi uma intengo ibuya.
• “Yini enyakaza kanye nayo?”: izici ze-coin eyodwa – imakethe yonke, ivolumu, ukuguquguquka, funding ne-open interest, ibanga kokuphezulu/okuphansi, Fear & Greed. Kubonisa okunyakaza ngasikhathi sinye – hhayi imbangela, izindaba, iseluleko sokutshala imali.
• Ayikho i-akhawunti, izikhangiso noma ukulandelelwa. Unjiniyela akatholi idatha yakho.

IDATHA EQOTHO
• “Konga” kuzenzakalela: izintengo zivuselelwa ekuvuleni i-app nangezikhathi ezithile ngemuva. “Bukhoma uma ivuliwe” yengeza izintengo ezibukhoma (Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase, Kraken) uhlu luvuliwe.
• Ushintsho lwe-% namashadi kuvela kuqala ku-exchange yepheya; uma ingenawo ama-candle: Binance noma Coinbase, futhi uyatshelwa.
• Fear & Greed, dominance namanani e-on-chain: alternative.me, CoinGecko, Coin Metrics.
• Amanani aphuma kudivayisi kuphela ngokuthumela kwakho noma ngesipele se-Android. Ezintengweni, ama-ticker kuphela aya ku-exchange yawo; nezinga lokushintshisana (Frankfurter), idatha yemakethe namalogo (CoinGecko, Binance), izibuko zedatha (GitHub). Uma ubuka amakheli/i-xpub, ama-node e-blockchain omphakathi abona amakheli (ku-xpub, akhiqiziwe) ne-IP yakho.

UHLU LOKUBUKA
• Izimakethe ezingu-56 kuma-exchange angu-45: spot nama-futures, amathokheni e-DEX nge-DexScreener, uma uthanda izitoko, igolide ne-FX (TradFi)
• Umugqa wesibili “≈ inani” ngemali eyodwa kwezingu-31 (EUR, CHF, USD …) ngezinga le-FX
• Ishadi elincane noshintsho lwe-% ku-24 h noma kusukela ngo-00:00 ngesikhathi sakho
• Amaqembu, izintandokazi, hudula-uyeke nenothi ku-coin ngayinye
• Imigqa iphawula “kudala” noma “i-exchange ayitholakali”

AMA-ALAMU
• Intengo ngaphezu/ngaphansi kwezinga, noma ukwenyuka/ukwehla ngephesenti – nangemali yakho, isb. 60,000 EUR kupheya le-USDT
• Ukunyakaza kuka-x % esikhathini, ukweqa kwevolumu
• Umsindo (nefayela lakho), ukudlidliza nokufunda ngokuzwakalayo uma uthanda
• Amahora athule no-“Hlola i-alamu”: zwa kuqala ukuthi ifika kanjani; ilogi: kuhlolwe nini, ngayiphi intengo, kungani ingakhalanga

IMAKETHE

OKUNYE
