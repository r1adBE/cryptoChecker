Get an alert when Bitcoin hits your price – from the exchange you actually use. Your exchange's price, not an average – free, no account.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
• Your exchange, not an average: each row shows the last price of the pair on the exchange you picked – Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, Bybit and many more.
• An alert that notifies once: “Tell me when BTC goes above 100,000 USDT.” It rings when the level is crossed, not on every update – “Once” then switches it off, “Every time” re-arms it when the price comes back.
• “What is moving with it?”: a factor list for one coin – overall market, volume, volatility, funding and open interest, distance to the high or low, Fear & Greed. It shows what is moving at the same time – no cause, no news, no investment advice.
• No account, no ads, no tracking. The developer receives none of your data.

HONEST ABOUT THE DATA
• “Economical” is the default: prices refresh when you open the app and at intervals in the background. “Live while open” adds live prices for Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase and Kraken while the watchlist is open.
• % change and charts come from the pair’s own exchange first; if it has no candles, the app uses Binance or Coinbase and tells you.
• Fear & Greed, dominance and on-chain values from alternative.me, CoinGecko and Coin Metrics.
• With the app closed, Android decides when alerts are checked – about every 15 minutes, often less frequently. Short spikes in between can be missed; the app tells you so.
• Amounts stay on your device unless you export them yourself (CSV, backup). For prices, only pair tickers go to the exchange of the respective pair; plus exchange rates (Frankfurter), market data and logos (CoinGecko, Binance), data mirrors (GitHub). If you watch addresses or an xpub, public blockchain nodes see the addresses (for an xpub, the derived ones) and your IP.

WATCHLIST
• 41 markets on 32 exchanges: spot and futures, DEX tokens via DexScreener, optionally stocks, gold and FX (TradFi)
• Second line “≈ value” in one of 31 currencies (EUR, CHF, USD …), converted at the FX rate
• Mini chart and % change over 24 h or since 00:00 in your time zone
• Groups, favorites, drag & drop and a note per coin
• Rows flag “outdated” or “exchange not reachable”

ALERTS
• Price above or below a level, or a rise or fall by a percentage – also in your currency, e.g. 60,000 EUR on a USDT pair
• Move by x % within a time window, volume spike
• Sound (also your own audio file), vibration and optional spoken announcement
• Quiet hours and “Test alert”: hear in advance how an alert arrives; alarm log: when it was checked, which price, why an alert didn’t fire

MARKET
• Unusual Activity: unusual price and volume moves in your watchlist, optionally as a market notice
• Crypto Pulse, Fear & Greed, Bitcoin indicators, funding and open interest – as plain measured values
• Network fees for Ethereum, Bitcoin and more, with gas alert

MORE
• Widgets: watchlist, portfolio total, single coin with chart
• Portfolio optional (turn it on in Settings): buys, sells, swaps, transfers, rewards, deposits and withdrawals; FIFO (default) or average (Ø); read-only addresses without a seed for BTC (incl. xpub), ETH & EVM chains, SOL, TRX, XRP, LTC and TON; CSV import from Relai, Swissquote, Bitpanda, Kraken, Binance and Coinbase; year overview as CSV; its own lock. Bitpanda and Swissquote have no public price feed, so they have no price of their own – import their trades via CSV.
• Readable for everyone: green/red or blue/orange, sign and arrow instead of color alone; TalkBack reads each row as one sentence
• Backup & restore to a file – also between Android and iPhone
• Light and dark mode, five accent colors, 31 languages
• Open source (MIT) – the code is public on GitHub

Note: Crypto Checker shows prices and a portfolio that you enter yourself. It is not an exchange or a wallet, does not trade and does not give investment advice.
