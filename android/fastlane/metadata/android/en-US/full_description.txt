Crypto Checker explains what is happening in the market. Set alarms, spot unusual moves and understand in seconds why a coin is moving – no forecast.

Understand – “Why is this moving?” with a short summary
Overview – Crypto Pulse, Fear & Greed, cycle and portfolio
Watch – prices straight from the exchange, in your currency
Alert – price, %, moves and volume, as a simple sentence

Readable for everyone: green/red or blue/orange price colors, high contrast, sign and arrow instead of color alone, and TalkBack reads every row as one sentence.

ALARMS ON YOUR TERMS
• Price above or below a level, or a rise or fall by a percentage
• Alarms in your currency, e.g. at 60,000 EUR on a USDT pair
• Alarm as a sentence: “Tell me when BTC falls below 60,000 CHF.”
• Move by x % within a time window, e.g. 5 % in 1 hour
• Volume spike: trading volume suddenly far above average
• Once or repeating, with sound, vibration and optional spoken announcement
• Your own alarm sound: system sound or your own audio file
• Adjustable quiet time prevents alarm floods
• Quiet hours: silent alarms at night (e.g. 11 pm–7 am), no sound, vibration or speech
• “Test alarm”: hear and feel in advance how an alarm arrives

PRIVACY FIRST
No account, no ads, no analytics. Prices come directly from the public APIs of the exchanges. Your watchlist, alarms and holdings stay on your device. Open source (MIT) – the code is public on GitHub.

Note: Crypto Checker only displays prices. It is not an exchange or a wallet, does not trade and does not give investment advice.
